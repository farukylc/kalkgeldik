Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

This policy explains how Merge Math ("the app") handles information. In short: we do not collect any data.

Data we collect

None. The app does not connect to the internet, does not require an account, and contains no advertising, analytics or tracking tools. As the developer, we have no access to any information about you.

What stays on your device

Your level progress, stars, best move counts, settings (language, theme, sound) and your hint, solution and undo counters are stored only on your device. They are never sent anywhere and are removed when you delete the app.

Level codes and the clipboard

When you share a level you made, the code goes through the iOS share sheet only to the app and people you choose; it never reaches us. When you tap "Paste" on the Enter Code screen, the clipboard text is read only at that moment to fill in the code and is not stored.

Purchases

The app is free and has no in-app purchases.

Children

The app does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Any updates to this policy will be published on this page with the date of the update.
